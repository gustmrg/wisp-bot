# Workspaces and storage

Each Wisp has a private folder, its workspace. Its file tools work only
there, and attached files land in its `inbox/` folder.

## A Wisp's workspace

The Wisp's [Workspace](wisp://wisp/workspace) settings show how much space it
uses, open the folder (in the desktop app on the computer that runs the
Wisps), and choose its **Size**. A workspace above its size keeps its files,
but no new files fit until you free space. **Inspect and clean up** opens it
in Storage.

## Storage

[Storage](wisp://settings/storage) lists every workspace with its usage.
Open one to see which folders take the space, and remove files permanently
after a cleanup preview. **Archived** lists the files of deleted Wisps, which
you can delete permanently.
