# Notifications, appearance and shortcuts

## Notifications

[Notifications](wisp://settings/notifications) turns **Notification sounds**
on or off, sets the volume, and chooses which events play a sound: **Response
completed**, **Approval needed** and **Execution error**. **Mute the open
conversation** silences the conversation on screen. Each Wisp also has its own
**Notifications** switch in its [Profile](wisp://wisp/profile), and a
conversation can be muted from the conversation list. Only sounds are
available for now, not system notifications.

## Appearance and system

[General](wisp://settings/general) has the theme (system, light or dark),
**Wisp colors** (off keeps a neutral look), **Launch at login** (Linux
releases only), and the timezone used for every date in the app and by Wisps.

## Shortcuts

[Shortcuts](wisp://settings/shortcuts) lists the built-in shortcuts and lets
you change the one for voice input. Built in: **Ctrl+K** search, **Ctrl+B**
collapse the sidebar, **Ctrl+Shift+Enter** schedule a message (**Cmd** instead
of **Ctrl** on a Mac).
