# Conversations and the composer

## The composer

- **Attach files** — from the **+** button. Files are copied into the Wisp's
  workspace `inbox/` folder; up to 20 per message, within the workspace size.
- **Voice input** — the microphone button dictates a message. It needs a
  transcription key; see the *Voice input* topic.
- **Schedule send** — from the **+** menu, a long press or right click on the
  send button, or **Ctrl+Shift+Enter** (**Cmd+Shift+Enter** on a Mac). The
  message is sent at the chosen time.
- **Queue message** — while the Wisp is working, a new message waits in a
  queue and is sent when the current reply ends.
- **Stop response** — stops the reply in progress.
- **Start a new topic** — from the **+** menu: the next message starts
  without the earlier context. See the *Context and memory* topic.

## Approvals

When a Wisp needs permission, for example to change a file or call an
integration, an approval card appears in the conversation and the sidebar
shows **Waiting for your approval**. See the *Access and approvals* topic.

## The conversation list

Right-click a conversation to pin it, mute its notifications, or mark it as
read or unread. **Ctrl+K** (**Cmd+K**) opens the search dialog, which searches
Wisps, circles and messages. **Ctrl+B** (**Cmd+B**) collapses the sidebar on
the desktop.

Circles, conversations with several Wisps, are not enabled yet.
