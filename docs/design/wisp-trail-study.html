Trails: the tail as a family trait.

Every Wisp is a body and a trail. The trail is what sets a Wisp apart from a ball with eyes, and it stays readable at 24 px. The agent's state moves the trail and the eyes, so the conversation list shows what each Wisp is doing. The app implements this study; see ADR 010.

Specimen

Real sizes 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 48 · cluster

24 px is the compact avatar, 32 px the sidebar row, and a circle's cluster draws each member at about 17 px. Below 32 px the mark goes and the eyes grow a little; below 28 px an outline fills in.

Body × trail click to choose

Can Wisps be told apart at this size? “Silhouette only” removes color and eyes to check that the shape holds up alone.

Sidebar states 32 px · collapsed on the left

The first row uses the chosen recipe and state. The others show a varied family, each in a state.

WorkingThe trail sways, the eyes look around, and the body floats.
Waiting for approvalAn amber dot in the bottom corner, like the failure one, and the eyes grow.
FailedA red dot, faded color, the trail down, and eyes looking down. Clears when the conversation is opened.
UnreadStill the dot in the top corner. The Wisp looks as usual.

Palette: vivid × soft 24 px · dark and light

Each palette color has a soft tone derived from it: the same color, lighter, not a second palette.
